## 📖 Special Notes

### About Sunan An-Nasai

Sunan An-Nasai is one of the six canonical hadith collections in Islam (Kutub as-Sittah), compiled by Imam Ahmad an-Nasai (214-303 AH). It contains authentic hadiths with the highest standards of verification and is widely respected in Islamic scholarship.

This application aims to make this invaluable resource accessible to everyone seeking authentic Islamic knowledge.

### For Scholars & Students

- Comprehensive cross-reference capability
- Detailed hadith chain analysis
- Authentication status clearly marked
- Multiple language comparisons
- Perfect for academic research

### For General Users

- Easy-to-understand interface
- No Islamic knowledge prerequisites
- Daily hadith inspiration
- Personal spiritual growth tool
- Family-friendly content
