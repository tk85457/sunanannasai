## LONG DESCRIPTION

### Main Description
Welcome to **Sunan An-Nasai** – your comprehensive digital companion to one of Islam's most authentic hadith collections. This professional-grade application brings the complete Sunan An-Nasai (Sunan of An-Nasai) directly to your fingertips, available in Arabic, Urdu, and English. Whether you're a student of Islamic knowledge, a researcher, or a devoted believer seeking guidance, this app provides everything you need for deep exploration and personal study.

Developed with meticulous attention to Islamic scholarship and modern user experience, Sunan An-Nasai offers seamless navigation, powerful search capabilities, and beautiful typography specifically optimized for Arabic and Urdu texts. All content is stored locally on your device, ensuring you have complete access to the entire hadith collection offline, anywhere, anytime.

## DETAILED FEATURES

### 📚 1. Complete Sunan An-Nasai Collection
The app contains the entire, unabridged Sunan An-Nasai hadith collection, one of the six most reliable hadith compilations (Kutub as-Sittah) in Islam. Every hadith is accurately preserved with its original chain of narrators (isnad) and authentication status, maintaining the scholarly integrity of this classical Islamic work. You have instant access to thousands of authentic hadiths organized by chapters (Kitabs) and topics for easy navigation and study.

### 🌐 2. Multi-Language Support (3 Languages)
Study hadiths in your preferred language:
- **Arabic**: The original text in beautiful, authentic Arabic
- **Urdu**: Complete Urdu translations for Urdu-speaking learners
- **English**: Precise English translations for international readers

Switch between languages instantly while reading any hadith, allowing comparative study and deeper understanding across different translations and interpretations.

### 🔍 3. Advanced Search Functionality
Find exactly what you're looking for with powerful search tools:
- **Keyword Search**: Search by any word or phrase across the entire collection
- **Exact Match**: Find precise phrase matches in hadiths
- **Partial Match**: Discover hadiths containing related terms and concepts
- **Sahih Filter**: Filter results to show only authenticated (Sahih) hadiths
- **Multi-Language Search**: Search across all three languages simultaneously
- **Pagination**: Browse through search results efficiently with 30 hadiths per page
- **Real-Time Results**: Get instant results as you type

Perfect for researchers, students, and anyone seeking specific hadiths on particular topics or keywords.
