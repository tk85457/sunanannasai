## 🌟 About Sunan An-Nasa'i

**Sunan An-Nasa'i (سنن النسائي)**, compiled by **Imam Ahmad ibn Shu'ayb an-Nasa'i (rahimahullah)**, is renowned for its strict criteria of authenticity and detailed legal chapters on Taharah, Salah, and Sunnah.

This Flutter app delivers the complete **Sunan An-Nasa'i** collection in **3 languages (Arabic, Urdu & English)** with fast offline search and dark mode.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📜 **Complete Collection**: All books and chapters of Sunan An-Nasa'i.
- 🌐 **3 Languages**: Original **Arabic**, **Urdu** (سنن النسائي اردو), and **English** translations.
- ⚡ **Offline Search**: Search by Hadith number or keywords in all 3 languages.
- 📡 **100% Offline**: Embedded database with no internet requirement.
- 📌 **Bookmarks**: Save important Hadiths and study notes.
